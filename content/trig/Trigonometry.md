# Trigonometry

Identities and the machinery for rewriting waves. Part of [[Home]].

## Notes
- [[compound angle formula]] — $\sin(A\pm B)$, $\cos(A\pm B)$, $\tan(A\pm B)$, two derivations, and everything that falls out of them (double angle, half angle, product-to-sum, the $R$-formula).

## Why it matters here
- **Integration:** the power-reduction identities are the only way into $\int\sin^2$ and $\int\cos^2$ — see [[trig|trig integration]].
- **Oscillators:** the $R$-formula turns the two-constant solution $C\cos\omega t + D\sin\omega t$ into a single amplitude-and-phase wave $A\sin(\omega t + \theta)$. That step is used in [[dampened harmonic oscillator]] and explains the shape of the solutions in [[Complex conjugate roots]].
- **Geometry:** the rotation-matrix derivation is a composition of two rotations in [[Euclidean space]].
- **Chain rule:** the factor of $a$ on $\sin(ax)$ throughout comes from [[chain-rule|the chain rule]].
- **Meshing:** the $(\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$ ring in [[Cylinder]] and the two-angle sweep in [[Sphere]] are the unit circle turned into vertex buffers; the $90°$ case of the compound angle formulae fills a trig table by rotation instead of evaluation.
