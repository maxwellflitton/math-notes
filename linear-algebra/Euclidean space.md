Euclidean space is an n-dimensional space but the "Euclidean" part is a specific claim about how distance and angles work in it. For Euclidean space the distance is calculated by the shortest straight path. Distance using Pythagorean theorem works, parallel lines never meet, angles in a triangle sum to 180°. We can calculate distance with the following:

$$
distance = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2}
$$
